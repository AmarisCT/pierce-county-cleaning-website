Standard clean

For homes kept in regular shape. Typical time: 2 to 5 cleaner-hours depending on size.

Kitchen

  • Countertops and backsplash wiped
  • Sink and faucet scrubbed and shined
  • Stovetop and appliance exteriors cleaned
  • Microwave inside and out
  • Cabinet fronts spot-cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Bathrooms

  • Toilet cleaned inside, outside and base
  • Shower, tub and tile scrubbed
  • Sink, counter and mirror cleaned
  • Fixtures shined
  • Floors mopped
  • Trash emptied

Bedrooms & living areas

  • Reachable surfaces dusted
  • Beds made (linens changed if left out)
  • Mirrors and glass tables cleaned
  • Light switches and door handles wiped
  • Carpets vacuumed, hard floors mopped
  • Trash emptied

Deep clean

Everything in the standard clean, plus the detail work that builds up over months. Recommended for a first visit or if it has been more than a month since a professional clean.

  • Baseboards, door frames and doors hand-wiped
  • Window sills and tracks
  • Light fixtures and ceiling fan blades dusted
  • Soap scum and hard-water build-up in bathrooms
  • Behind and under movable furniture and appliances
  • Kitchen cabinet fronts fully wiped

Move-in / move-out

For empty homes before a new tenant, after you leave, or before listing. Includes the deep clean list plus the inside of cabinets, drawers, fridge and oven. Homes must be empty of belongings and have working water and power.

We follow a standard move-out checklist. We can't guarantee a deposit return, because each landlord sets their own standard.

Recurring service

Weekly, biweekly or monthly standard cleans at a lower per-visit price. We try to send the same cleaner each time. Homes that haven't been professionally cleaned recently start with a deep clean.

Not included

  • Exterior windows, gutters and pressure washing
  • Carpet shampooing and upholstery cleaning
  • Mold, biohazard, hoarding or fire and flood cleanup
  • Moving furniture heavier than one person can safely move
  • Pet waste cleanup beyond normal floor cleaning
  • Ladder work above two steps
