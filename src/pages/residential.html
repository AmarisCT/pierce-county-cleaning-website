Standard clean

For homes kept in regular shape. Kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas, cleaned top to bottom.

{{photo name="res-standard" tone="sand" class="dots" alt="Freshly made bed with white linens" note="made bed, white linens"}}

The checklist

Kitchen

  • Countertops and backsplash wiped
  • Sink and faucet scrubbed and shined
  • Stovetop and appliance exteriors
  • Microwave inside and out
  • Cabinet fronts spot-cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Bathrooms

  • Toilet inside, outside and base
  • Shower, tub and tile scrubbed
  • Sink, counter and mirror
  • Fixtures shined
  • Floors mopped
  • Trash emptied

Bedrooms & living

  • Reachable surfaces dusted
  • Beds made (linens changed if left out)
  • Mirrors and glass tables
  • Switches and door handles wiped
  • Carpets vacuumed, floors mopped
  • Trash emptied
Recommended for a first visit

Move-in / move-out

Before a new tenant, after you leave, or before listing. The deep clean list plus the inside of cabinets, drawers, fridge and oven. The home must be empty, with water and power on.

We follow a standard move-out checklist, but each landlord sets their own deposit standard.

Recurring service

Standard cleans on a schedule, at a lower price per visit. We try to send the same cleaner each time. Homes that haven't had a professional clean recently start with a deep clean.

Not included

  • Exterior windows, gutters and pressure washing
  • Carpet shampooing and upholstery cleaning
  • Mold, biohazard, hoarding, fire or flood cleanup
  • Moving furniture heavier than one person can safely move
  • Pet waste beyond normal floor cleaning
  • Ladder work above two steps
