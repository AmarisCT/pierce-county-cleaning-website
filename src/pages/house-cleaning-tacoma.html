Older homes

Character takes detail

Many homes in the North End, Stadium and Hilltop are close to a century old, with painted trim, built-ins, original wood floors and deep window tracks. Dust settles into all of it.

For a first visit we suggest a deep clean, which hand-wipes baseboards, door frames, sills and tracks. Standard or biweekly visits keep it there after that.

Good to know

Apartments & condos

For buildings downtown and in the Stadium District, tell us about parking, entry and any elevator rules when we confirm. Please don't type door codes into the quote form.

Moving out

A move-out clean covers the inside of cabinets, drawers, fridge and oven, the places landlords check first.

Pay after

We email an invoice after your clean. Pay by card, Zelle, cash or check.

Tacoma questions

Do you clean wood floors in older homes?

Yes. Finished wood floors get a damp mop, never a soaking. If you use a particular floor cleaner, leave it out and note it on your booking.

My building has no visitor parking. Can you still come?

Usually, yes. Tell us when we confirm your booking. We'll plan around it and agree on any parking cost with you before the appointment.

Do you clean offices in Tacoma too?

Yes, small offices, shops and salons. Business cleaning is priced after a walkthrough. See office and commercial cleaning.
