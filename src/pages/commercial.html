How commercial quotes work

  1. Tell us about the space

    Square footage, visits per week, hours you need us, and anything special.

  2. Walkthrough

    We visit, note every room and surface, and agree on what is in scope.

  3. Written scope and price

    You get a statement of work and a monthly price before anything starts.

  4. Quality checks

    Each visit follows the agreed checklist, with a named contact for issues.

What we don't offer yet

We do not currently take medical or clinical facilities, post-construction cleanup, floor stripping and waxing, or any hazardous-material cleanup.
