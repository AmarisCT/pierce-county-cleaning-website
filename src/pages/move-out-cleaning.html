The move-out checklist

Kitchen

  • Inside fridge and freezer
  • Inside oven
  • Inside cabinets and drawers
  • Countertops, backsplash and sink
  • Stovetop and appliance exteriors
  • Microwave inside and out
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Bathrooms

  • Toilet inside, outside and base
  • Shower, tub and tile scrubbed
  • Hard-water build-up on fixtures
  • Inside vanity cabinets and drawers
  • Sink, counter and mirror
  • Floors mopped

Every room

  • Baseboards and door frames hand-wiped
  • Window sills and tracks
  • Light fixtures and fan blades
  • Switches and door handles
  • Carpets vacuumed, hard floors mopped

Not included: carpet shampooing, wall painting or patching, exterior windows, and anything else on our not-included list.

Before we arrive

1

Empty it

Everything out, including the fridge, cabinets and closets. We can't clean around belongings.

3

If your landlord gave you a move-out checklist, send it with your booking and we'll compare it with ours.

4

Photos after

Take dated photos of each room when we finish. They help if a deduction is ever disputed.

For Washington renters

Deposit basics

Under Washington law, your landlord has 30 days after you move out to return your deposit or give you a full and specific statement of what they're keeping and why, with copies of estimates or invoices for damage. They can't keep any of it for wear from ordinary use, or for carpet cleaning unless they document wear to the carpet.

General information, not legal advice. Read RCW 59.18.280 or ask a tenant resource about your situation.

Move-out questions

Will a move-out clean get my deposit back?

No cleaner can promise that, because each landlord sets their own standard. We clean to the checklist above. If your landlord flags something on our checklist within 24 hours of the clean, we come back within 72 hours to fix it at no charge.

Do you clean before a move-in, too?

Yes. It's the same clean, done while the home is empty and before your things arrive.

Do I need to be there?

No. Many customers leave a key or lockbox. Access details go only to your assigned cleaner.

Do you shampoo carpets?

No. We vacuum carpets thoroughly. If your lease requires professional carpet cleaning, book a carpet cleaner separately, after our clean.

Do you clean for landlords and property managers?

Yes, including turnovers between tenants. Property managers with several units can be billed per unit. See rentals and turnovers.
