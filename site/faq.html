Questions

Frequently asked

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. Cleaners bring all supplies and equipment. If you prefer a specific product, leave it out and note it on your booking.

How is the price set?

By home size, the type of clean and any add-ons. The quote form shows an estimate, and we confirm the final price before booking. Heavy build-up, very large homes or heavy pet shedding may need a custom quote.

How soon can you come?

Choose a preferred date on the quote form. We confirm the closest available slot, usually within the same week.

How do I pay?

After your clean, we email you an invoice. Pay by card or by Zelle, cash or check, as listed on the invoice. Recurring customers get an invoice after each visit.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes, free with at least 48 hours' notice. With less notice, or if we can't get in on arrival, a fee of up to 50% of the visit price may apply.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many customers leave a key, lockbox or door code. We store access details securely and share them only with your assigned cleaner.

What about pets?

We're happy to clean around friendly pets. Tell us about them when you book, and secure any pet that may be anxious or slip out an open door.

What if something is missed?

Tell us within 24 hours, with photos if you can. We'll come back within 72 hours to re-clean the missed checklist items at no charge.

What if something is damaged?

Tell us right away. We document what happened and repair, replace or reimburse according to our damage policy.

Should I tip?

Tips are never expected. If you choose to tip, all of it goes to your cleaner.
