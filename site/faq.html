Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. Cleaners bring all supplies and equipment. If you prefer a specific product, leave it out and note it on your booking.

How is the price set?

By home size, the type of clean and any add-ons. The quote form shows an estimate, and we confirm the final price before booking. Heavy build-up, very large homes or pets with heavy shedding may need a custom quote.

How soon can you come?

Choose a preferred date on the quote form. We confirm the closest available slot, usually within the same week.

How do I pay?

By card. We authorize your card when you book and charge it after the clean is complete. Recurring customers are billed after each visit.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes, free of charge with at least 48 hours' notice. Cancellations with less notice, or if we can't get in on arrival, may be charged a fee of up to 50% of the visit price.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many customers leave a key, lockbox or door code. We store access details securely and share them only with your assigned cleaner.

What about pets?

We're happy to clean around friendly pets. Please tell us about them when you book, and secure any pet that may be anxious or may slip out an open door.

What if something is missed?

Tell us within 24 hours, with photos if you can. We'll come back within 72 hours to re-clean the missed checklist items at no charge.

What if something is damaged?

Tell us right away. We document what happened, and we repair, replace or reimburse according to our damage policy.

Do you tip?

Tips are never expected. If you choose to tip, 100% goes to your cleaner.
