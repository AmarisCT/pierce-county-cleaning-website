About us

A local cleaning company built on a checklist

Clean Spaces, Clear Minds. A clean home frees up your evenings and weekends. We want booking it to be just as easy.

How we run

Pierce County Cleaning Services is a family-run company managed here in Pierce County. We handle quotes, scheduling, billing and customer care. Our cleaners are our employees, trained on the same checklist and supplied with our products and equipment.

We would rather give you a firm price up front than surprise you afterward. We would also rather tell you what we don't do than take on work we can't do well.

Our standards

Same checklist every time

Every room is cleaned in the same order, and the cleaner signs off each section.

Prices confirmed first

You approve the price before the appointment is booked.

Problems fixed quickly

Report a missed task within 24 hours and we return within 72 hours.

Respect for your home

Keys and entry codes are stored securely and shared only with your assigned cleaner.
