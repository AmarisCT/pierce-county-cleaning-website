Clean Spaces, Clear Minds. A clean home gives you back your evenings and weekends. Booking it should be just as easy.

How we run

Pierce County Cleaning Services is family-run and managed here in Pierce County. We handle quotes, scheduling, billing and customer care. Our cleaners are our employees, trained on the same checklist and supplied with our products and equipment.

We would rather give you a firm price up front than surprise you afterward, and we would rather tell you what we don't do than take on work we can't do well.
