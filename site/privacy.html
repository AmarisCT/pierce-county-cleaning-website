Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

What we collect

When you request a quote on our website, we email you a copy of your quote. When you request a quote or book a clean, we collect your name, phone number, email, service address, details about your property, and any notes you give us. When you pay by card, your card details go directly to our payment processor. If you choose, we also collect home access instructions after your booking is confirmed.

How we use it

  • To prepare quotes, schedule appointments and send reminders
  • To give your assigned cleaner the address and instructions they need
  • To process payments and keep business and tax records
  • To follow up on quality and ask for feedback

What we don't do

We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.

Home access details

Keys, lockbox codes and door codes are stored securely with restricted access. They are shared only with the cleaner assigned to your appointment and are deleted when you end service.

Service providers

Quote requests are stored in our Google account (Google Sheets) and quotes are emailed to you from our business Gmail. Card payments are processed by Stripe, and the website is hosted by GitHub. We may add a scheduling service as we grow. They process information only to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep customer and payment records as long as needed for service and for Washington tax and business record requirements, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us. We'll respond within 30 days.
