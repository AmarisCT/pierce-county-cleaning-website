Privacy policy

Last updated: [date of publication]

What we collect

When you request a quote on our website, we email you a copy of your quote. When you request a quote or book a clean, we collect your name, phone number, email, service address, details about your property, and any notes you give us. When you book, we also collect payment information through our payment processor and, if you choose, home access instructions.

How we use it

  • To prepare quotes, schedule appointments and send reminders
  • To give your assigned cleaner the address and instructions they need
  • To process payments and keep business and tax records
  • To follow up on quality and ask for feedback

What we don't do

We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers. Card numbers are handled by our payment processor and are never stored by us.

Home access details

Keys, lockbox codes and door codes are stored in our scheduling system with restricted access. They are shared only with the cleaner assigned to your appointment and are deleted when you end service.

Service providers

Quote requests are stored in our Google account (Google Sheets) and quotes are emailed to you from our business Gmail. We also use outside services for website hosting and, when used, scheduling and payments. They process information only to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep customer and payment records as long as needed for service and for Washington tax and business record requirements, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us. We'll respond within 30 days.
