Biweekly cleaning

Busy households often book a standard clean every other week, at a lower price per visit. We try to send the same cleaner each time, so they learn your home.

Homes that haven't had a professional clean recently start with a deep clean, then move to the biweekly price.

Good to know

Bigger homes

Many South Hill homes have three or four bedrooms. Our price table runs to 4 bedrooms and 2.5 baths; larger homes get a custom quote before booking.

Rainy-season floors

From fall through spring, entries and mudrooms take the most wear. Note it on your booking and it goes on your cleaner's checklist.

Pets

We're happy to clean around friendly pets. If heavy shedding changes the time needed, we'll tell you before the price is final.

Puyallup questions

Do you clean on South Hill?

Yes. South Hill is part of our Puyallup area, including ZIP codes 98373, 98374 and 98375.

My home is bigger than 4 bedrooms. Can you still quote it?

Yes. Send a quote request with your bedrooms and bathrooms and a note about the size of the home. We'll send a custom price before anything is booked.

Can I add extras to a biweekly visit?

Yes. Inside the fridge, oven or cabinets, interior windows, detailed baseboards or a load of laundry can be added to any visit. See add-ons and prices.
