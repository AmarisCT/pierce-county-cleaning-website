Terms of service

Last updated: [date of publication]. These terms apply to residential cleaning. Commercial clients receive a separate written service agreement.

Quotes and pricing

Online estimates assume a home in typical condition. We confirm your price before booking. If conditions on arrival differ substantially from the request (for example, heavy build-up or a larger home than described), we will contact you before continuing, and you may approve a revised price or cancel without charge.

Payment

We authorize your card when your booking is confirmed and charge it after the clean is complete. Recurring visits are charged after each visit. Unpaid balances more than 15 days past due may pause recurring service.

Rescheduling and cancellation

You may reschedule or cancel free of charge with at least 48 hours' notice. With less notice, or if our cleaner can't access the home at the scheduled time, we may charge up to 50% of the visit price. We will give you as much notice as possible if we need to reschedule.

Re-clean promise

If a task on our service checklist was missed or not completed to standard, notify us within 24 hours of the clean, with photos where possible. We will return within 72 hours to re-clean the affected areas at no charge. The promise covers checklist tasks for the service you booked. It does not cover areas that changed after the cleaner left, tasks outside the booked service, or conditions we noted at the time of service, such as permanent stains or damage. If a re-clean doesn't resolve the issue, we will discuss a partial refund for the affected areas.

Access to your home

You are responsible for providing access at the scheduled time. Keys and codes are stored securely and shared only with your assigned cleaner. Please secure valuables, cash and fragile heirlooms before your clean.

Damage and breakage

Report any damage within 24 hours. We will document it, and if we are responsible we will repair, replace or reimburse the reasonable cost, at our option. We are not responsible for damage caused by pre-existing wear, improperly installed items, or items we were not told were fragile or valuable.

Safety

Our cleaners may decline tasks that are unsafe, including ladder work above two steps, moving heavy furniture, or cleaning biohazards, mold or pest infestations. Pets must be secured if they may be aggressive or escape.

Our staff

Cleaners are employed by Pierce County Cleaning Services LLC. To protect our team, please don't hire our cleaners directly for private work during service or for 12 months after.
