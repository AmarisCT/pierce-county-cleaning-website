Lakewood questions

Can you guarantee I'll get my deposit back?

No cleaner can promise that, because each landlord sets their own standard. We clean to a detailed move-out checklist. If your landlord flags something on our checklist within 24 hours of the clean, we come back within 72 hours to fix it at no charge.

Can I pay after I've moved away?

Yes. We email the invoice after the clean, and you can pay by card or Zelle from anywhere.

Do you clean apartments, or only houses?

Both. Apartments are priced by bedrooms and bathrooms, the same as houses. If your community gave you a move-out list, send it with your booking and we'll compare it with ours.
