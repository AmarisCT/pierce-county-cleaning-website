University Place questions

Can my real estate agent book the clean?

Yes. Whoever books gets the quote and invoice. Let us know who will let the cleaner in, or arrange a lockbox.

How far ahead should I book before listing photos?

Request a quote as soon as your photo date is set, and book the clean for a day or two before it.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many customers leave a key, lockbox or door code. We store access details securely and share them only with your assigned cleaner.
