# Draft launch pricing

**Status: pending owner approval.** These prices feed `src/assets/prices.js`.

Built from the cost model in `internal/pricing-engine.js`: W-2 cleaner at $22/hour, about 12.5% payroll burden (Social Security/Medicare, unemployment, paid sick leave), L&I class 6602 at about $1.68 per hour worked (2026 composite base rate), 30 minutes of paid drive time, 10 miles at $0.70, $8 supplies, card fees of 2.9% + $0.30, $1,500 a month of overhead over 40 jobs, and an assumed 1.75% B&O rate (verify with your accountant). Targets: about 25% net on one-time cleans, about 20% on biweekly.

Market check: Tacoma standard cleans typically run about $120–$250 (NW Maids 2026 PNW cost index). These prices sit in the upper half because cleaners are paid as employees.

## The old $40 first deep clean

Not recommended. A 2-bed deep clean costs about $164 in direct labor, mileage and supplies, so a $40 price loses roughly $160 per job after overhead. A biweekly customer nets about $50 a visit, so it takes three to four visits to recover. Alternative: **$40 off a first clean booked with recurring service.**

## Sales tax

Routine house and janitorial cleaning: Service & Other B&O, **no retail sales tax**. Post-construction and other special cleanups are retail sales and do carry sales tax; the site does not offer them.
