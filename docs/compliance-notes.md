# Compliance notes that affect the website

Not legal advice. Confirm with an attorney or accountant before launch.

- **Staffing claims.** The owner chose independent contractors (Nov 2026). The site says the company partners with independent cleaners who bring their own supplies (`index.html`, `about.html`, `terms.html`). Keep it that way: don't describe cleaners as employees or staff, and don't set their hours.
  - L&I: a 1099 has no bearing on Washington workers' comp coverage. Each cleaner must pass all six parts of L&I's test to be exempt, or the business owes workers' comp premiums for them. Confirm the setup with L&I or an attorney before the first job. https://lni.wa.gov/independentcontractor
- **Sales tax.** Routine house cleaning is reported under Service & Other B&O and isn't subject to retail sales tax. Post-construction cleanup is a retail sale. https://dor.wa.gov/education/industry-guides/janitorial-and-other-cleaning-services/construction-cleanup-services
- **Insurance and bonding.** Do not say "insured" or "bonded" anywhere until certificates are in hand.
- **Reviews.** Google prohibits incentivized or fake reviews. The site has no testimonials; add real ones only with customer permission.
- **Terms to review:** 50% late-cancellation fee, damage reimbursement "at our option", and the 12-month staff non-solicitation clause.
- **Privacy.** The quote form collects names, phone numbers, emails and addresses. Use a form service with HTTPS and private storage, and don't collect access codes through the website.
